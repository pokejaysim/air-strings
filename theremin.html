How to play

Pitch hand

Hold one hand up on the right side. Move it left–right to change the note. Wobble it quickly for vibrato. Move it up for a brighter tone.

Press and drag on the right side. Move left–right to change the note.

Volume hand

Your other hand on the left: raise it for louder, drop it to the bottom for silence. With one hand up, you play at a steady volume.

On a touchscreen, a second finger on the left sets the volume: higher is louder.

Assist

Off is a real theremin (hard!). Gentle pulls you toward the scale. Snap always lands on a note.

Learn teaches you a song: first find each note at your own pace (the song waits for you), then follow the ribbon in time for stars. Listen plays the song for you first.

Pinch and hold a toolbar button for a moment to press it (your left hand is quietest for this). Range sets the octaves: Low C2–C4, Mid C3–C5, High C4–C6.

Face (F): after a second to learn your resting face, open your mouth for a brighter "wah", round your lips into an "oo" for a darker sound, and raise your eyebrows to jump up an octave (free play only).

Hand signs, held for a moment: ✊ fist silences the theremin while you hold it · 👍 says yes to the big button in a lesson · 👎 starts a lesson song again · ✌️ switches to Air Strings · 🤟 confetti!
