How to play

Draw

Pinch thumb + index, drag, and let go to lay down a string.

Click and drag, then release to lay down a string.

Play

Pinch a string, pull it sideways, and let go to pluck. Or swipe your fingertip fast across strings to strum.

Drag a string sideways and release to pluck. Or drag fast across strings from empty space to strum.

Pitch

Shorter = higher, longer = lower (E2 to E5). Pinch a toolbar button to press it.
