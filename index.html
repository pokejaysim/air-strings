Air Strings

Draw guitar strings in the air with your hand, then pluck them. Short strings ring high, long strings ring low.

Uses your webcam for hand tracking. Video never leaves your device.

How to play

1 · Draw

Pinch thumb + index in empty space, hold until the ring fills, then drag and let go.

Click and drag in empty space, then release to lay down a string.

2 · Pluck

Pinch a string, pull it sideways, and let go. Pull further for louder.

Drag a string sideways and release. Pull further for louder.

3 · Strum

Swipe an open hand fast across strings. Fingers touching mid-swipe won’t draw.

Flick fast across strings, starting from empty space.

Short strings ring high, long strings ring low (E2 to E5). Pinch a toolbar button to press it.
